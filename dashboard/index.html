How this ranks

Every number on the board follows from the sealed DefiLlama snapshot alone — TVL, seven-day change, category, chains and listing date. No protocol name is special-cased; the same function scores every row.

implied = TVL × peer multiple × growth × maturity × breadth
  • Peer multiple — the median market-cap/TVL of the tokenized protocols in the same category with at least $25M of TVL (a ratio off a tiny base is noise, not a market price), shrunk toward the global median (0.129) by sample size and clamped to 0.02–2.5. The market, not a guess, prices a dollar of TVL.
  • Growth — 1 + clamp(7d change, −50%, +100%) × 0.5. TVL moving toward a launch counts more than TVL standing still.
  • Maturity — 0.7 + min(age, 3 years) / 3 years × 0.3. A long record with no token prices years of work.
  • Breadth — 1 + 3% per extra chain, up to 1.15. More chains, more users who can farm it.

The signal

The airdrop-likelihood signal (0–100) is a heuristic from the same fields, never a prediction of a launch: TVL scale 0–30 (log-scaled $5M–$1B), seven-day momentum 0–20, age with no token 0–25, the category's points culture 5–15, chain breadth 0–10. High is 55+, Medium 40–54, Low below 40.

The board

The board holds 200 rows: every protocol on the snapshot's watchlist (40 protocols at or above the $50M floor that record no token and are not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of one that trades), plus the 160 strongest pre-token protocols below the floor by TVL. Rows whose TVL is escrowed, wrapped or bridged value carry a flag in their detail view. Farm plans are generated from each protocol's own category, chains, TVL and age, with a cost and a time on every step.
